Interpretation

The model combines quantitative procurement analysis with game-theory concepts to support negotiation preparation.

The quantitative layer establishes supplier alternatives, BATNA, reservation value, ZOPA, should-cost, TCO and Value-at-Stake.

The game-theory layer adds Nash bargaining, signalling, credibility, information asymmetry, timing / hold-up analysis and Shapley-based negotiation lever attribution.

These analytical outputs are converted into a practical buyer strategy containing an opening position, target price, walk-away boundary and supplier-specific negotiation actions.

Important:

This capstone uses synthetic/sample procurement data for demonstration and modelling purposes. The results should not be presented as actual Knorr-Bremse supplier findings or real company data.
